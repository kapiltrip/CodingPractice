[All topics](../README.md) · [Import and evidence notes](../maintenance/IMPORT.md)

## What this topic practises

Practise separate state-register, next-state, and Moore-output logic using an overlapping 101 detector. Follow-up work requests a synchronous vending-machine controller accepting Rs. 1 and Rs. 2 coins: dispense at Rs. 3, and dispense with Rs. 1 change at Rs. 4. No student RTL or testbench is present in the working topic folder yet. Official detector templates and supplied solutions are excluded.
